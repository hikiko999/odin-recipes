Fried Egg

Description

The classic fried egg is easy to make and is especially delicious with bacon or almost anything really.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole egg
  • 1 tablespoon of cooking oil of your choice
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • Black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Heat the frying pan to medium-high until you can feel the heat by placing your hand above it.
  2. Crack the egg and put it in the frying pan.
  3. Wait at least a minute or so. Fry for longer if you want it crispy.
  4. Add the pinch of salt
  5. If you want it sunny side up, you can leave it be like this, else turn it over.
  6. (OPTIONAL) Add salt to the flipped side.
  7. Add pepper to taste.
