Hotdog

Description

The classic Hotdog is easy to prepare and you can do it with a frying pan.

Ingredients

  • Hotdogs of your choice
  • 1 tablespoon of cooking oil of your choice

Steps

  1. Make slits on the defrosted hotdog. This helps it cook inside. You can use a knife or fork.
  2. Heat the frying pan to medium-high until you can feel the heat by placing your hand above it.
  3. Put the hotdog in the frying pan.
  4. You want to keep pushing and rolling the hotdog with a spatula or tongs every 5 seconds so it doesn't burn.
  5. Repeat this process for the rest of your hotdogs. Add more oil if necessary.
