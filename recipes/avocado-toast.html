Avocado Toast

Description

Avocado Toast is easy to make and pairs well with a fried egg.

Ingredients

  • 1 slice of bread (of your choice)
  • 1/2 ripe avocado
  • Salt to taste
  • (OPTIONAL) Pepper, chili flakes, or a drizzle of olive oil

Steps

  1. Toast your slice of bread to your desired crispiness in a toaster or on a pan.
  2. While the bread is toasting, cut the avocado in half, remove the pit, and scoop the flesh into a small bowl. Mash it with a fork until it's smooth or slightly chunky, whichever you prefer.
  3. Once the toast is ready, spread the mashed avocado evenly on top of the warm toast.
  4. Sprinkle a pinch of salt over the avocado. You can also add pepper, chili flakes, or drizzle a little olive oil for extra flavor.
